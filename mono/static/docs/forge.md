# Forge

**Question builder.** Forge is where you compose and schedule exams — from
scratch or from existing templates — with language-aware tooling.

## What it does

Forge provides a calendar-based workspace for planning exam sessions and
assembling their content. The language selector (FR / NL / DE) keeps prompts and
labels consistent with the audience you are authoring for.

## Using Forge

1. **Choose the language** (FR, NL or DE) from the selector in the page header.
   This drives the language-aware labels throughout the builder.
2. **Work in the calendar** to lay out and schedule your exams across dates.
3. **Compose** each exam from scratch or start from an existing template.

## Tips

- Set the language first — it affects how content is presented as you build.
- Forge focuses on authoring and scheduling; use **Export** to render finished
  exams to PDF/JSON and **Audit** to validate them against a source workbook.
